By day, I am a postdoctoral researcher at [DESY Hamburg](https://connect.helmholtz-imaging.de/expert/239), working on neural operator theory, spectral methods, and sparsity-driven representations 🧠📐.
By night, I build small tools and side projects—sometimes useful, sometimes unnecessary, usually interesting 🌙🛠️

**Projects**

* **[FlowSpeak](https://github.com/aqibrahimbt/flow_speak)** 🗣️🌱 — A companion app for building speaking confidence for people who stutter. Based on a structured year-long program combining breathing exercises, speech practice, reading sessions, mindfulness, and real-world challenges.
* **Circulate Book Swap (iOS & Android)** 📚🌍 — A book-swapping platform that lets readers exchange physical books and follow each book’s journey as it passes from hand to hand.
* **[Repro Audit](https://github.com/aqibrahimbt/repro_audit)** 🔍📄 — A deterministic reproducibility auditor for academic papers and codebases. It statically analyzes papers and repositories to surface missing seeds, undocumented hyperparameters, data leakage risks, and environment gaps.
